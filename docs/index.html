Command Center

Outreach metrics that drive real music placements.

Monitor your full campaign pipeline in real time. Track open rates with invisible 1x1 pixels, monitor positive replies, and enforce daily send caps against Gmail SMTP thresholds so your sender domain always stays healthy.

Curator CRM

Visual Kanban for journalists, playlisters, and A&Rs.

Organize every music contact through To Contact, Sent, Opened, Replied, Follow-up, and Blacklisted stages. Search across publications, filter by target personas, or batch-import contacts directly from CSV.

Pitch Composer

Unique pitches for every recipient. No spam hashes.

Dynamic spintax rotates subject lines, compliments, and streaming links so every curator receives a customized, natural message. Live Apple Mail and Gmail preview lets you inspect the rendered output and test-dispatch to your own device.

Intelligent Dispatcher

Human-like pacing with anti-spam safeguards.

Outreach shouldn't look like a machine. PitchFlow introduces randomized 3–7 minute intervals between emails and automatically holds batches over weekends to prevent Monday morning trash pile-ups.

Credential Vault

Zero .env hassle. Configured and verified in 60s.

Configure your artist profile, Gmail App Password, and optional Notion CRM database directly in the interface. Built-in SMTP and Notion diagnostic buttons test your live connection before sending a single pitch.

Core Capabilities

Engineered for music tastemakers.

Fast enough to launch in seconds. Safe enough to protect your reputation for years.

Spintax Variation Engine

Randomized sentence permutations prevent ESP finger-printing and spam blacklists. Every curator receives a distinct email.

Weekend Inbox Blocker

Automatic Saturday & Sunday suppression avoids Monday morning inbox purge routines, maximizing open rates on Tuesdays.

IMAP Opt-Out & Reply Detector

Scans your inbox to automatically celebrate positive replies and instantaneously blacklists contacts requesting removal.

Attachment Safety Filter

Protects you from spam traps by enforcing streaming URLs and cloud EPK kits instead of heavy MP3 attachments.

Notion CRM Dual-Sync

Optionally syncs your contacts and campaign updates directly to your Notion workspace for team-wide collaboration.

100% Local & Private

Zero telemetry. Runs locally via Python FastAPI with SQLite storage. Your contacts and credentials never leave your PC.
